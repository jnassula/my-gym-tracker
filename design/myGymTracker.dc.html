1myGymTracker — gym tracker PWA · shadcn/ui on the Nocturne theme
Assumptions
  • Sample data is your real PDF: Periodização de Treino 01 — Aluno: Jonata, 6 days (Thu = rest). Sample "today" is Wednesday so the week shows two days already done.
  • UI copy: PT-PT primary. The prototype has a live PT / EN switch (Settings → Idioma); exercise names stay as written in the PDF.
  • Nocturne → shadcn tokens: background #161826 · card #232532 · primary #9184d9 · muted-foreground #9397ab · border #3f424d · destructive #e07a86 · radius 0.625rem. Light mode swaps the ramps; dark is default.
  • Nocturne prefers outlined primaries. I keep variant="outline" for most actions and reserve a filled primary for the one hero action per screen (Registar série).
  • Weight steps +5 … +25 as briefed; a long-press on ± gives 2.5 kg for dumbbells (flagged custom).
  • Icons: Phosphor (placeholder glyphs in these mocks).
Design reasoning
  • Mid-workout ergonomics: every tap target ≥ 48px, weight at 64px, one-thumb reach — the log button and the ± steppers live in the bottom third.
  • Fewest interactions: opening an exercise pre-fills weight from the last session and reps from the plan; one tap logs a set and starts the rest timer automatically.
  • Set logging is a shadcn Drawer so history and chart stay visible underneath.
  • Bottom tabs: Hoje (jumps straight into today's day), Treinos, Progresso, Definições. Desktop: same four in a sidebar, list + detail in two columns.
  • Custom pieces flagged: weight stepper, mini sparkline in list rows, calendar heatmap, watch mockups.
